Account & Data Deletion

Unknown is anonymous — there is no email/phone account. Your identity is a random device ID plus data stored on your own device. Here is how to delete it.

Delete your data inside the app

  1. Open Unknown → Settings (⚙️).
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap 🗑 Delete my data and confirm.

This permanently erases your name, profile, friends, saved chats and settings from the device, and starts a fresh anonymous ID.

Or simply uninstall

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. Because your chats are end-to-end encrypted and never stored on our servers, nothing personal remains after uninstall.

Server-side data

Our servers keep only anonymous safety data (a random ID, last-seen timestamp, and moderation records such as reports/bans). To request deletion of any server-side data tied to your anonymous ID, email us:

support@unknown.app — include your Account ID (Settings → Account). We delete it within 30 days.

We never collect your email, phone number or real name. Chat content is end-to-end encrypted and is never stored.
