Child Safety Standards · CSAE Policy

Unknown has a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), and toward any content or behaviour that endangers a minor. This page describes our standards, as required by Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

Unknown is strictly for adults (18+). The app is not directed at children, and minors are not permitted to use it. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 on the service.

1. Zero tolerance for CSAE

We prohibit, and act on, any child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or child sexual exploitation, including grooming, sextortion, solicitation of a minor, trafficking, or sharing/attempting to share such content. There are no exceptions.

2. How users report abuse

  • In-app: every chat has a ⚑ Report and Block control. Reports are actioned by our moderation system.
  • Auto-ban: a user reported by multiple people is automatically banned by account and network/IP.
  • Email: to report child-safety concerns directly, contact unknownapp.support@gmail.com with the reported user's Account ID. We aim to respond within 24 hours.

3. How we respond

  • We review reports promptly and remove offending users, permanently banning their account and network/IP.
  • We preserve relevant evidence and report apparent CSAE to the appropriate authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or local law enforcement, as required by law.
  • We cooperate with lawful requests from child-protection agencies and law enforcement.

4. Safety measures in the app

  • 18+ age gate on first launch.
  • Report & Block in every conversation; multi-report auto-ban.
  • Received photos are blurred by default until the viewer chooses to reveal them.
  • A word filter blocks certain harmful content before it is sent.
  • Community rules prohibit nudity, sexual content involving minors, harassment, and illegal activity.

5. Child safety point of contact

Child-safety matters are handled by our safety contact at unknownapp.support@gmail.com. We comply with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and applicable CSAE laws in the regions where the app is available.

6. Compliance

This policy reflects our commitment to preventing CSAE on our platform and to meeting the standards set out in Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and relevant legislation.
