Unknown
Privacy Policy · Effective 7 September 2026

Unknown is an anonymous 1-on-1 chat app. We built it privacy-first: we do not ask for your identity and we cannot read your conversations. This policy explains, plainly, what we do and don't collect.

The short version
  • No email, phone number, or real name required.
  • Your chats and shared media are end-to-end encrypted — our servers only relay scrambled data they cannot read.
  • Most of your data (name, friends, saved chats) lives only on your own device.
  • 18+ only.

1. Who you are to us

When you first open the app you choose a display name. The app also generates a random anonymous ID for your device (for example u_ab12cd…). This ID is not linked to your real identity, phone, email, or Google account. It is used only to route messages, show friends' online status, let friends reconnect, and enforce moderation (reports/bans).

2. What is stored on your device only

The following stay in your device's local storage and are never sent to our servers (they are lost if you clear app data or uninstall):

  • Your display name, chosen gender, orientation, bio, and profile photo.
  • Your friends list and your saved chats with friends.
  • App settings (theme, language, notifications, last-seen preference) and your subscription status.

3. What our servers handle

  • Your anonymous device ID and chosen display name (to connect you and route messages).
  • Basic, non-identifying attributes you set — gender and orientation — used for optional matching and anonymous statistics.
  • Your last-seen timestamp, unless you turn "Show my last seen" off.
  • Moderation records: report counts and bans, keyed to the anonymous ID (and, for banned users, network/IP information to enforce the ban).

We do not store the content of your chats, photos, voice messages, or calls.

4. End-to-end encryption

Your 1-on-1 text chats and shared media (photos, voice messages) are end-to-end encrypted using a key exchange (ECDH P-256) and AES-GCM encryption performed on the two devices. Voice and video calls use WebRTC, which encrypts media (DTLS-SRTP) directly between devices. Our server only passes along encrypted data and cannot decrypt or read it.

5. Media & camera/microphone

Photos and voice/video use your camera, gallery, and microphone only when you choose to send media or start a call, and only with your permission. Media in chat is end-to-end encrypted and relayed peer-to-peer or via our relay — it is not stored on our servers. For safety, received photos are blurred by default until you choose to view them.

6. Safety & moderation

  • Every chat has Report and Block. A user reported by multiple people is banned by anonymous ID and network/IP.
  • A word filter on your device blocks messages that appear to offer or sell illegal drugs before they are sent.
  • Our moderators can see only anonymous metadata — how many people are online, gender/online status, report counts — and can block or message users. They cannot read your chat content, which stays end-to-end encrypted.

7. Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing — processes PRO subscription payments. We never see your card details.
  • Google AdMob — shows banner and video ads inside the app. To do this, Google AdMob collects and processes your device's Advertising ID and related device/technical information to serve and measure ads (personalized or non-personalized). In regions that require it (e.g. the EEA/UK), you are shown a consent choice before ads load. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Ads privacy policy. PRO subscribers may see fewer or no ads.
  • STUN/TURN servers — help establish voice/video calls.

These providers have their own privacy policies, which apply to their processing.

8. Your choices

  • Turn off "Show my last seen", or use Ghost mode to appear offline.
  • Block or unfriend anyone; manage blocked users in Profile.
  • Delete all your data at any time by clearing app data or uninstalling.

9. Age requirement

Unknown is strictly for users 18 and older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

10. Data retention

Device data remains until you remove it. On our servers, your anonymous ID is kept while your account is active; last-seen and moderation records are retained to keep the service safe. We do not retain chat content.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the effective date above.
